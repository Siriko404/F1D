### Tested and ruled out (16):
4. **DV formula**: Table 8 uses cheq/(atq_lag - cheq) — "net of cash holdings." Table 1 uses cheq/atq_lag. Confirmed correct. Table 1 DV gives δ≈0.
5. **Winsorization order**: Winsor cheq/atq_lag THEN derive final ratio = correct. Reversing (derive then winsor) kills result (δ=−0.036).
6. **R² gap**: FE-inclusive (paper's 0.21) vs within-R² (our 0.03). Reporting convention, not model problem. Confirmed via Stata/reghdfe documentation.
7. **Full-distribution tercile**: Bottom third is entirely negative (T1=−0.078). Gives 0 control. Does not reproduce paper's 360 control.
8. **Log-vol specification**: Cutpoints 0.12/0.25 — further from paper's 0.28/0.68. Level-vol confirmed correct.
9. **Data sources**: Bloomberg FTSE100/S&P500/FX vs Yahoo/CRSP/BoE. Identical by direct comparison of underlying series. Not the issue.
10. **RET vs RETX**: 99% identical daily returns, negligible.
11. **SHRCD/EXCHCD filter**: Adding CRSP share code 10/11 + exchange 1/2/3 filter. Overshoots on HIGH count (424→too few), marginal on LOW.
12. **Rolling vol**: Subscript t in eq (13) implies within-month SD. Rolling/overlapping windows not supported by paper.
13. **RHS vol construction**: Already consistent with LHS (all within-month SD of daily returns).
14. **Min-days/min-months sweep**: 25 combinations tested (MinDays∈{10,13,15,17,20} × MinMon∈{12,18,24,30,36}). T2 ranges 0.62→0.69, best at MinDays=10/MinMon=12 (T2=0.6894). T1 never reaches paper's 0.28 — maxes at 0.265.
15. **I/B/E/S asymmetric attrition** (tested today): HIGH firms are LESS analyst-covered (19.1% missing I/B/E/S) than LOW firms (8.6% missing). Median NUMEST: HIGH=7, LOW=9. ALL stricter I/B/E/S criteria (CUSIP-only, NUMEST≥3, NUMEST≥5) drop MORE HIGH firms than LOW, making H/L ratio worse. Paper's H/L=1.25 asymmetry cannot come from I/B/E/S.
16. **CF beta (beta^UK_i,CF)** (attempted twice): Vuolteenaho (2002) firm-level VAR(1) on quarterly [r, ROE, BM], 2002-2018, ≥30 quarters. v1 (≥12 quarters): rank corr 0.14 with baseline beta. v2 (≥30 quarters, 3,484 firms): rank corr 0.22, top-tercile overlap 22.9%. Paper: rank corr 0.80, overlap 86%. CF news SD=30.5 vs baseline SD=1.3 — inherently noisy at firm level. Cannot validate construction without paper's exact method.

### Key finding: negative beta proportion
Our betas on Compustat survivors: 2,349 firms, 1,431 nonnegative (60.9%), 918 negative (39.1%). Full-distribution T1=−0.078 (entirely negative bottom third). The paper's cutpoints (0.28/0.68) and reported counts (449 treated, 360 control) imply they have ≤~20% negative betas. Distribution shape fundamentally different on identical inputs.

### SE invariance argument (RA Round 4)
Same N, same SE (0.060 vs 0.059), same Var(X) (treatment proportion ~0.50 in both) → same σ_resid → DV scale/units/winsor are correct. But Cov(Y,X) is 2.3x smaller → TREATMENT COMPOSITION differs. Different firms land in treated/control groups.

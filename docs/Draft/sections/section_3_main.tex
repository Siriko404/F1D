\section{Main Empirical Analyses}
\label{sec:main}

\subsection{Data, Sample, Variables, and Specification}
\label{sec:main:setup}

We construct a quarterly panel of 112{,}968 earnings-call transcripts covering 2{,}429 unique firms over fiscal years 2002--2018, sourced from S\&P Capital~IQ. Call-level speech metrics are merged with Compustat quarterly fundamentals and CRSP daily security data. Following \citet{bates2009}, we exclude financial firms (SIC 6000--6999) and regulated utilities (SIC 4900--4999); we refer to this filtered universe as the Main sample throughout.

The dependent variables are CashRatio (\texttt{cheq/atq}, the linear cash-to-assets form adopted by \citealt{bates2009}), Leverage (\texttt{(dlttq+dlcq)/atq}), and DebtToCapital. The primary speech-uncertainty regressor is UncAnsMgr, a call-level measure of manager Q\&A uncertainty constructed from the \citet{dzielinski2021} uncertainty wordlist applied to the all-manager pooled aggregation in the spirit of \citet{bushee2018}; full construction details appear in \S\ref{sec:framework}. Base controls are lnAssets, TobinsQ, ROA, Capex, DivDummy, sCFO, and a unit-by-DV Lagged~DV. Extended controls add SalesGrowth, RDSales, CashFlowAt, and DailyVola. The reciprocal control rule applies: leverage enters as a control in cash regressions, and cash enters as a control in leverage regressions.

We estimate PanelOLS specifications across a four-step fixed-effects ladder (Industry $+$ Calendar Year, Firm $+$ Calendar Year, Industry $+$ Calendar Year-Quarter, Firm $+$ Calendar Year-Quarter), with standard errors clustered at the firm level. Each main suite reports twelve columns: contemporaneous and one-quarter-lead dependent variables crossed with the FE ladder. Inference for the IV in this section follows the one-tailed convention pre-committed in \S\ref{sec:framework}.

\section{Additional Analyses}
\label{sec:additional}

\subsection{Drivers of CEO Speech Uncertainty}
\label{sec:additional:drivers}

The cash-financing and outside-world results in Sections~\ref{sec:main} and \ref{sec:additional:reaction} treat the speech-uncertainty constructs as right-hand-side regressors. Their interpretation as legitimate uncertainty proxies depends on whether they respond, on the left-hand side, to independently measured exogenous uncertainty drivers. We test four such drivers as construct-validation regressions of the speech-uncertainty constructs on (i) the firm-level political-risk index of \citeA{hassan2020}, (ii) the U.S.\ news-based economic-policy-uncertainty index of \citeA{baker2016}, (iii) the global extension of \citeA{davis2016}, and (iv) product-market competition pressure as measured by the text-based total similarity index of \citeA{hoberg2016}. Each driver regression is reported across twelve cells: the six speech-uncertainty constructs (Manager-pool, CEO-only, and non-CEO-manager partitions, each at the prepared-remarks and Q\&A segment) crossed with two entity fixed-effect configurations (industry vs.\ firm). The macro-driver suites (BBD-EPU and GEPU) use two-way clustering on firm and calendar quarter to handle within-quarter cross-firm correlation in the macro regressor, per Section~\ref{sec:framework:design}. The four drivers test construct validity rather than formal hypotheses.

\textbf{Political risk (Hassan et al., 2020).} The contemporaneous firm-level political-risk index loads positively on every speech-uncertainty construct in all twelve cells of Table~\ref{tab:h11_prisk_uncertainty} at $p<0.01$ one-tailed, with point estimates between $+0.0001$ and $+0.0003$. The two-quarter-lag specification (Table~\ref{tab:h11_lag2}) is positive in all twelve cells with eleven of twelve significant at $p<0.05$ and eight of twelve at $p<0.01$, point estimates between $+0.0000$ and $+0.0001$. The lag specification establishes that the firm-level political-risk signal is a leading driver of within-call CEO speech uncertainty, not merely a contemporaneous correlation.

\textbf{Macro economic-policy uncertainty (Baker, Bloom, and Davis, 2016; Davis, 2016).} The U.S.\ economic-policy-uncertainty index (Table~\ref{tab:h24_us_epu}) loads positively on all twelve speech-uncertainty cells, with seven of twelve significant at $p<0.10$ (five of twelve at $p<0.05$ and two of twelve at $p<0.01$), point estimates between $+0.0006$ and $+0.0239$. The global extension (Table~\ref{tab:h24b_global_epu}) is similarly positive in all twelve cells, with nine of twelve significant at $p<0.10$ (five of twelve at $p<0.05$, three of twelve at $p<0.01$), point estimates between $+0.0053$ and $+0.0309$. The two macro indices both deliver the predicted construct-validation pattern: the speech-uncertainty constructs covary with independently measured macro uncertainty.

\textbf{Product-market competition (Hoberg and Phillips, 2016).} The text-based total-similarity index loads positively in five of twelve cells at $p<0.05$ and three of twelve at $p<0.01$ in Table~\ref{tab:h23_competition_uncertainty}. Per-cell inspection of the suite specification shows the five significant cells are uniformly positive with point estimates between $+0.0090$ (Manager-pool Q\&A, industry FE) and $+0.0304$ (CEO-only Pres, industry FE); the seven cells not statistically distinguishable from zero include three with negative point estimates, all of them non-significant at $p<0.10$. The competition signal therefore loads in the predicted direction wherever it loads, with the strongest effects on the prepared-remarks segment under industry fixed effects (point estimates of $+0.0297$ for Manager-pool Pres and $+0.0304$ for CEO-only Pres). The Pres-channel concentration is consistent with \citeauthor{dzielinski2021}'s framing of the prepared-remarks segment as the firm-culture-reflecting share: competition pressure manifests in the segment of the call constructed in advance with the firm's broader competitive context in mind.

\textbf{Synthesis.} All four uncertainty drivers load positively in every cell that is statistically distinguishable from zero, with $p<0.10$ significance covering 12, 7, 9, and 5 of 12 cells for political risk, US-EPU, GEPU, and competition respectively. The three NS-negative cells in the competition specification (cols 7, 8, and 12 of Table~\ref{tab:h23_competition_uncertainty}, all with $p>0.50$ one-tailed) are noise rather than direction reversal. The political-risk lag specification confirms timing. Sign and significance evidence across the four drivers establishes the speech-uncertainty constructs as legitimate uncertainty proxies before the cash-financing and outside-world outcome tests in Sections~\ref{sec:main} and \ref{sec:additional:reaction}.

ClarityCEO & CEO clarity persistent-style component (DWZ Eqn 5).\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} ClarityCEO$_i$ = $-\widehat{\mathrm{FE}}_i$ where $\widehat{\mathrm{FE}}_i$ is the CEO-$i$ fixed-effect estimate from the panel regression UncAnsCEO$_{ic}$ = $\alpha + \sum_i \mathrm{FE}_i \cdot \mathbb{1}[\text{CEO}=i] + \mathbf{X}_{ic}\boldsymbol\gamma + \varepsilon_{ic}$ over the call-level Q\&A uncertainty panel; controls $\mathbf{X}$ are the DWZ extended-controls vector. The sign flip on $\widehat{\mathrm{FE}}_i$ makes higher ClarityCEO mean ``CEO speaks more clearly'' (lower mean uncertainty). Persistent CEO trait. & \seqsplit{.../ceo\_clarity\_extended/} & \citet{dzielinski2021} (Section 4.4 Eqn 5, p.16) \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
ClarityCEO\_QtrExp & ClarityCEO under within-tenure expanding window.\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} As ClarityCEO, but the CEO fixed-effect regression is re-estimated on an expanding within-CEO window using only calls of CEO $i$ available up to and including focal call $c$. \addlinespace[2pt]
NegCall & Entire-call negative sentiment percentage.\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} Ratio of negative words to total words in the entire call, based on LM 2011 negative-words list. Per DWZ 2021 Appendix Table A.1: 'percentage of negative words in all words spoken by the CEO, CFO and analysts attending the call.' & \seqsplit{outputs/2\_Textual\_Analysis/2.2\_Variables} & \citet{dzielinski2021} (Section 4.4, p.18; Appendix Table A.1, p.54) \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
UncAnsCEO & CEO-only Q\&A uncertainty percentage.\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} CEO Q\&A uncertainty (\%) = UnctWordsAnsCEO / WordsAnsCEO (DWZ 2021 Eqn 2). Uncertainty wordlist = LM 2011 Master Dictionary 'uncertainty' list (297 words, Aug 2014 version). CEO identified via Execucomp ceoann field. & \seqsplit{outputs/2\_Textual\_Analysis/2.2\_Variables} & \citet{dzielinski2021} (Section 4.2, Eqn 2, p.13; Appendix Table A.1, p.54) \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
UncPreCEO & CEO-only presentation uncertainty percentage.\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} CEO Presentation uncertainty (\%) = UnctWordsPreCEO / WordsPreCEO (DWZ 2021 Eqn 1). Uncertainty wordlist = LM 2011 (297 words). & \seqsplit{outputs/2\_Textual\_Analysis/2.2\_Variables} & \citet{dzielinski2021} (Section 4.2, Eqn 1, p.13; Appendix Table A.1, p.54) \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
UncQue & Analyst Q\&A uncertainty percentage.\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} Analyst Q\&A uncertainty (\%) = UnctWordsQue / WordsQue (DWZ 2021 Eqn 3). Uncertainty wordlist = LM 2011 (297 words). & \seqsplit{outputs/2\_Textual\_Analysis/2.2\_Variables} & \citet{dzielinski2021} (Section 4.2, Eqn 3, p.13; Appendix Table A.1, p.54) \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
UncResCEO & CEO Q\textbackslash{}\&A residual call-state uncertainty component (DWZ Eqn 4).\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} $\widehat\varepsilon_{ic}$ = the residual from the panel regression UncAnsCEO$_{ic}$ = $\alpha + \sum_i \mathrm{FE}_i \cdot \mathbb{1}[\text{CEO}=i] + \mathbf{X}_{ic}\boldsymbol\gamma + \varepsilon_{ic}$ that produces ClarityCEO. By OLS first-order conditions, the within-CEO mean of UncResCEO is exactly zero by construction. Captures call-level deviation in CEO Q\&A uncertainty after stripping out the persistent CEO mean. & \seqsplit{.../ceo\_clarity\_extended/} & \citet{dzielinski2021} (Section 4.4 Eqn 4, p.16) \\
\addlinespace[2pt]
UncResCEO\_QtrExp & UncResCEO under within-tenure expanding window.\par\smallskip\textit{Formula:} As UncResCEO, but the residualization regression is re-estimated on an expanding within-CEO window. Strictly look-ahead-free. & \seqsplit{.../ceo\_clarity\_expanding/} & thesis QtrExp variant of \citet{dzielinski2021} Eqn 4 \\

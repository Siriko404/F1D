\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We extend the three-component CEO speech-uncertainty decomposition of \citeA{dzielinski2021}---a persistent CEO-style trait (\texttt{ClarityCEO}, the negative of a CEO fixed effect estimated from prepared-remarks segments), a call-level state residual within the Q\&A segment (\texttt{UncResCEO}), and a presentation-segment uncertainty share (\texttt{UncPreCEO})---from the market-reaction, analyst-behavior, firm-performance, and CEO-compensation outcomes on which \citeauthor{dzielinski2021} validate it to a class of outcomes they do not test: firm-level financing-policy decisions. Using a sample of $112{,}968$ earnings-call transcripts covering $2{,}429$ U.S.\ firms over fiscal years 2002--2018, we estimate the canonical precautionary-cash response of \citeA{opler1999} and \citeA{bates2009} on the three components jointly. The Q\&A residual \texttt{UncResCEO} loads positively on cash holdings in all twelve specifications of a four-step fixed-effects panel ($p<0.10$ one-tailed; eight of twelve at $p<0.05$ in the Full method, twelve of twelve at $p<0.10$ and seven of twelve at $p<0.05$ in the QtrExp expanding-window method), and persistent CEO style loads negatively in nine of twelve specifications under both methods. The state-residual response amplifies among credit-constrained Unrated firms on the one-quarter-lead dependent variable (two of eight cells at $p<0.05$ in the \citeA{faulkender2006} interaction), and among firms in the upper half of the cash-flow-volatility distribution \cite{hanqiu2007} at one-tier-weaker significance. Construct-validation regressions show CEO speech uncertainty itself responds positively to firm-level political risk \cite{hassan2020}, US and global news-based economic-policy-uncertainty indices \cite{baker2016,davis2016}, and product-market competition \cite{hoberg2016}. Outside-world reaction regressions show that the post-call quoted bid-ask spread (4 of 12 specifications at $p<0.10$) and SEC comment-letter receipt (4 of 6 specifications at $p<0.10$) both load \emph{presentation-segment} uncertainty---the segment \citeauthor{dzielinski2021} characterize as scripted firm-culture-reflecting---rather than the Q\&A components. Three identification designs (CEO sudden-death difference-in-differences, \citeauthor{dzielinski2021} \S6 first-difference replication around CEO turnover, and \citeA{lewbel2012} heteroskedasticity-based instrumental-variable estimation) address reverse-causality, time-invariant heterogeneity, and time-varying omitted-variable threats respectively.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} CEO speech uncertainty, earnings calls, precautionary cash holdings, financing conservatism, credit constraints, instrumental variables, textual analysis. \\[0.25em]

Evidence boundary

What the Evidence Does and Does Not Show

The evidence identifies a language pattern, not its cause.

What it shows

A readable pattern

CEO Q&A uncertainty tracks the point when an acquisition moves from private to public.

A within-firm regularity around disclosure - no more, and no less.

What it does not show - and where it may not carry
01
Cause and effect
The design is descriptive, not causal. It characterizes a within-firm regularity.
02
Generalizability
The sample skews toward larger, more heavily covered U.S. public firms (approximately the S&P 1500, 2002-2018), limiting generalizability.
03
A direct reading of the CEO
The finance-specific word count is an imperfect proxy that abstracts from context; it does not read the CEO's mind.
04
A perfect comparison
Stock deals share the disclosure setting, but remain an imperfect comparison for cash deals.

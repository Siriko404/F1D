Conceptual Framework I

Why Uncertainty Appears

01
Private acquisition
The firm holds potentially material deal information that investors do not yet have.
02
Routine Q&A call
The CEO still faces analysts in the less-scripted part of the earnings call.
03
No clean answer
The firm may stay silent, but once it speaks it cannot mislead.
04
Language trace
Answers may become more hedged, qualified, and uncertain before announcement.
Core prediction
CEO Q&A uncertainty should rise while the acquisition is still private.
Boundary
The framework predicts the language pattern. It does not identify whether silence is legally constrained or strategically chosen.
Thesis framework: Verrecchia (1983); Dye (1985); Basic (1988); Rule 10b-5; Matsumoto et al. (2011); Hollander et al. (2010)

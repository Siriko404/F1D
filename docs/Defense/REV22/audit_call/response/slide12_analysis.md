# Slide 12 analysis

## Bottom line

The four-box structure can remain, but boxes 3 and 4 need correction. Box 3 states a real limitation in the correct direction but stops before the inferential consequence. Box 4 retains both old limitations but gives them a technically misleading heading. The two planned oral rebuttals are not safe as currently framed.

## What the slide currently says

- Box 1: Cause and effect.
- Box 2: Generalizability.
- Box 3: "The data mark the announcement date, not when negotiations began."
- Box 4: "Imperfect instruments" covering the word-count measure and stock comparison.

## Box 3: the anticipatory window

### What is correct

The event clock is anchored on the public announcement. The supplied data description lists announcement, completion or withdrawal, and payment composition; it does not list negotiation onset. The old PRE2 guard was correctly removed because an e=-2 null cannot detect negotiations that begin after the e=-1 call.

### What is still wrong

The sentence identifies a missing date but does not say what follows from it. Some calls flagged PRE1 may occur before the relevant negotiation, materiality, withholding, or knowledge state begins. Those states are related but not identical. The slide therefore raises a serious objection without defining the narrower claim that survives it.

### Recommended wording

> Event time is anchored on announcement; negotiation onset is unobserved, so PRE1 is a proxy for the possible withholding window, not proof that every flagged call occurred during negotiations.

This wording is longer, but it prevents the candidate from implying observed exposure.

### Should the limitation be on the slide?

Only if the candidate is prepared to give the conditional answer below. Otherwise it is safer in Q&A notes. The current one-line version is the worst combination: it volunteers the issue while leaving the examiner to articulate its consequence.

## Box 4: the merge

The substantive merge does not lose the two thesis limitations. The word count abstracts from context; stock deals remain an imperfect counterfactual. They can occupy one box. The heading should not be "Imperfect instruments" because neither is an instrumental variable.

Recommended heading:

> Measurement and comparison limits

Recommended sentence:

> The finance-specific word count abstracts from context; stock deals share the disclosure setting but remain an imperfect comparison for cash deals.

## Rebuttal 1: "misclassification attenuates toward zero"

This is not established without assumptions.

It is defensible only if:

1. The observed PRE1 indicator mainly creates false positives among otherwise untreated calls.
2. Timing error is nondifferential after firm effects, calendar effects, and controls.
3. Misflagged calls have the same conditional mean outcome as ordinary quarters.
4. True exposure effects have a stable nonnegative sign.
5. Negotiation onset is not selected by deal complexity, stress, payment method, call timing, or latent CEO uncertainty.
6. The fixed-effects weighting and other event bins do not introduce offsetting contamination.

The thesis cannot verify these conditions because onset is unobserved. Differential timing can bias the estimate either way. The safe spoken answer is:

> Under classical, nondifferential contamination the coefficient would be attenuated. But onset is unobserved, so I cannot sign the bias. I interpret PRE1 as an announcement-anchored average association, not an identified effect of negotiation exposure.

## Rebuttal 2: "PRE2 proves tight timing and no pre-trend"

The reported PRE2 estimate is 0.0068 with SE 0.0178. Its approximate 95% interval is [-0.028, 0.042]. That result means there is no statistically detected average displacement at e=-2. It is not an equivalence test, does not exclude smaller earlier movement, and is only one lead. It also cannot address negotiations beginning after the PRE1 call.

Safe spoken answer:

> We do not detect elevation at PRE2, which is consistent with the pattern being concentrated in PRE1. It does not prove the absence of earlier drift, and it does not identify when negotiations began.

## Four-box decision

Keep four boxes if:

- Box 3 is rewritten precisely.
- Box 4 is renamed.
- The candidate discards the unconditional attenuation and no-pre-trend rebuttals.

Otherwise remove box 3 from the main deck and retain the issue in the Q&A preparation. Do not restore the PRE2 guard.

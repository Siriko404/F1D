# Subject Brief — Defense Presentation Master Reference Hard Scrutiny

## Audit kind

Rigorous factual, interpretive, structural, and presentation-content audit.

## Audited artifact

`docs/Defense/DEFENSE_PRESENTATION_MASTER_REFERENCE.txt`

The audit is read-only. No correction may be made to the audited artifact until the
panel has sealed its findings and the mechanical read-back gate has run.

## Exact question

Is the master reference a trustworthy and complete guide for building and delivering
Sina's 20-minute thesis-defense presentation, when checked against the flattened thesis,
the approved presentation map, the current rendered deck, and authoritative institutional
and branding sources?

## What a useful answer must establish

1. Every sample count, coefficient, standard error, p-value, confidence interval,
   variable definition, empirical-design statement, and robustness statement is accurate.
2. Every causal or interpretive claim stays within what the thesis design supports,
   including null results, generated-regressand caveats, timing evidence, scrutiny tests,
   and observational equivalence of mechanisms.
3. The 12-slide story is logically complete, nonredundant, correctly sequenced, and
   feasible within the stated 20-minute limit.
4. Each slide's purpose, on-screen content, narration, takeaway, timing, and transition
   agree with the overall argument and do not promise evidence the thesis lacks.
5. Committee, institutional-convention, source-link, Telfer-brand, typography, and visual
   production claims are accurate or explicitly labeled as judgment/inference.
6. The reference is internally consistent: no conflicting numbers, titles, timings,
   terminology, file status, or instructions appear in different sections.
7. Every reported defect is an atomic record with an exact quote from the reference,
   exact counter-evidence from a registered source when applicable, location, severity,
   reasoning, and a narrowly stated correction direction.
8. Material uncertainty or missing evidence is reported as a gap, never silently guessed.

## Pass bar

The reference passes only if:

- all panel journals seal and all required context is checked as read;
- the mechanical read-back gate accepts every grounded quotation;
- no unresolved CRITICAL or HIGH defect remains;
- all numerical/result claims checked by the results expert match their sources;
- all material interpretation and identification claims are correctly bounded;
- the timing total and slide-level allocations are arithmetically and practically coherent;
- all source-dependent institutional and branding claims are supported by saved raw sources;
- any MEDIUM or LOW issue is explicitly recorded rather than suppressed; and
- any facet that cannot be verified is marked as a gap and prevents an unqualified pass.

## Out of scope

- Rewriting the thesis.
- Re-estimating the empirical models from raw datasets.
- Editing the reference, HTML/CSS deck, PDF, thesis, or source files during this audit.
- Inventing new empirical results or visual evidence.
- Treating subjective design preference as factual error.
- Reviewing backup material that is neither specified in the reference nor needed to
  verify a claim made by it.

## Deliverable

Mechanically verified atomic findings only. No prose synthesis, silent conflict
resolution, or ungrounded reassurance. Corrections, if requested later, form a separate
post-audit task.

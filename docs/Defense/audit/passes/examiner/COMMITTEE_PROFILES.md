## Examiner: Dr. Shantanu Dutta, Telfer

Full Professor of finance, Ian Telfer Fellowship in Global Finance. Research
spans mergers and acquisitions, media coverage and financial decisions, corporate
governance, market efficiency, dividend policy, and machine learning and natural
language processing in financial decisions.

Selected work:

- Long-term performance of acquiring firms, Journal of Banking and Finance 2009
- Does payment method matter in cross-border acquisitions? IREF 2013. This is a
  method-of-payment paper, directly adjacent to this thesis's third analysis.
- Inside the black box of private in-house meetings, Review of Accounting Studies
  2018. Selective disclosure through private channels.
- Does governance quality influence insider trading around private meetings?
  Accounting Horizons 2023
- Does media coverage affect credit rating change decisions? JBF 2022
- Using 10-K text to gauge COVID-related corporate disclosure, PLOS ONE 2024
- CEO power and M&A, JMFM 2011; CEO tenure and M&A, FRL 2020; Are good performers
  bad acquirers? FM 2012; Do say-on-pay votes affect M&A decisions? JCF 2025

Methodological taste: event studies, panel corporate finance, textual analysis
with active interest in NLP and machine learning, media data, insider-trading
data.

Reading: he is the closest thing in the room to a domain expert on this thesis's
exact intersection, which is mergers and acquisitions crossed with textual
disclosure crossed with private information flows. Expect informed and specific
questions rather than generic ones. His own work on private in-house meetings is
directly relevant to whether an unobserved private channel could produce the
pattern. No public statement of his standards on causal identification was found.

Separately sourced and treated as reliable: he prefers defenses that finish
promptly. That argues for a tight, findings-forward delivery inside the time
limit, not for changing any content.

## Examiner: Dr. Rengong (Alex) Zhang, Telfer

Joined Telfer in 2024 in accounting, currently listed as Associate Professor. PhD
from the University of Alberta, previously at City University of Hong Kong.
Research covers big data and alternative data, workplace safety and labour
aspects of ESG, and capital markets. Teaches data analytics in accounting.

Selected work:

- Does Sunlight Kill Germs? Stock Market Listing and Workplace Safety, JFQA 2023
- Short-Selling Pressure and Workplace Safety, Organization Science 2023
- Media Co-coverage and Overreaction in Cross-Industry Information Transfers,
  EAR 2024
- Post-Earnings-Announcement Drift and Parameter Uncertainty, RQFA 2020. This one
  is uncertainty crossed with prices.
- Voluntary Risk Disclosures of Entrepreneurial Firms, 2025 preprint

Reading: an alternative-data and machine-learning methodologist with disclosure
and information-transfer interests, and a quasi-experimental taste in his own
identification strategies. Expect drilling on the data pipeline, on the choice of
a fixed word list over a modern language model, on media and information-transfer
confounds, and on whether the market sees what the measure sees. His work on
post-earnings-announcement drift and parameter uncertainty makes the price and
spread angle personal to him.

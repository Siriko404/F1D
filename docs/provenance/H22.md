## B. MODEL SPECIFICATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------

### B1. Regression Equation

EquityDelayCon_lead_{i,t+1} = alpha + beta_1 * UncAnsCEO_{i,t} + beta_2 * UncPreCEO_{i,t}
    + beta_3 * UncAnsMgr_{i,t} + beta_4 * UncPreMgr_{i,t}
    + gamma * Controls_{i,t} + Lagged_DV_{i,t} + FE + epsilon_{i,t}

Where IVs are fiscal-year averages of call-level uncertainty measures.

### B2. Dependent Variable

| Variable | Description | Formula | Source |
|----------|-------------|---------|--------|
| EquityDelayCon_lead | Next FY equity constraint | equitydelaycon(t+1) | Hoberg-Maksimovic |

Cosine similarity of 10-K CAPLIQ text to equity-delay training set (firms
delaying investment due to liquidity AND planning equity issuance), with
boilerplate purging. Mean ~ 0 by construction. Higher = more constrained.

### B3. Independent Variables

| Variable | Description | Level |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| UncAnsCEO | CEO Q&A uncertainty | Fiscal-year mean of call-level |
| UncPreCEO | CEO Presentation uncertainty | Fiscal-year mean of call-level |
| UncAnsMgr | Manager Q&A uncertainty | Fiscal-year mean of call-level |
| UncPreMgr | Manager Presentation uncertainty | Fiscal-year mean of call-level |

### B4. Controls

**Base:** lnAssets, TobinsQ, ROA, Leverage, Capex, CashRatio, DivDummy, sCFO, Lagged_DV
**Extended:** Base + SalesGrowth, RDSales, CashFlowAt, DailyVola

### B5. Fixed Effects

| Spec | Entity FE | Time FE |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Cols 1, 3 | Industry (FF12) | Fiscal Year |
| Cols 2, 4 | Firm | Fiscal Year |

### B6. Standard Errors

Firm-clustered (cluster_entity=True).

### B7. Hypothesis Test

One-tailed (beta > 0): higher uncertainty -> more equity constraints.

## E. DESIGN NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------

### E1. Firm-year unit (unique in pipeline)
This is the first firm-year panel in the pipeline. All other 25 suites
operate at the call level. The annual unit is necessary because the DV
(equitydelaycon) is annual — one score per firm per fiscal year from
the 10-K filing.

### E2. B1 fix: cal_yr = fyearq_int
The panel builder sets `cal_yr = fyearq_int` explicitly, NOT from
`start_date.dt.year`. This prevents misalignment for ~1% of firm-years
where the latest call falls in Jan-Mar of the next calendar year
(e.g., FY2010 Q4 call in Jan 2011 -> cal_yr=2011 != fyearq_int=2010).
Red-team audit identified this as BLOCKER B1.

### E3. Merge key verification
The constraints README says `year` = "calendar year in which the firm's
fiscal year ends." This is MISLEADING. Empirical testing confirmed that
constraints `year` = Compustat `fyearq` (the fiscal year identifier),
not the calendar year of fiscal year end. Proof: 425 non-Dec FY-end
firms with fyearq=2015 have 73% match at year=2015, while their
calendar year would be 2016 (impossible since data ends at 2015).

### E4. CashRatio as potential mediator (W2)
CashRatio is included as a control for consistency with all other suites.
However, it is potentially a mediator (uncertainty -> cash hoarding ->
lower equity constraint). Including it biases AGAINST finding significance,
making any significant results conservative.

### E5. Nickell bias (W3)
Lagged DV with firm FE on short panels (T~10) introduces Nickell (1981)
bias of ~-1/(T-1) ~ -10%. This is standard practice in empirical finance
and is required by the pipeline's lagged DV convention.

### E6. No winsorization
equitydelaycon has kurtosis=0.30 (platykurtic, lighter tails than normal)
with only 9 observations beyond 3-IQR bounds. No winsorization needed.

cash-deal-concentrated component: it rises in the quarter(s) before an undisclosed
cash acquisition and resolves at announcement — tracking the deal's disclosure
state, not the firm's cash balance or analyst scrutiny. Mechanism (constrained
MNPI disclosure / strategic silence) is a hedged interpretation, not a claimed
identification.

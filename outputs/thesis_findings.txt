CROSS-SUITE NARRATIVE
================================================================================
================================================================================

THE STORY: Linguistic uncertainty in earnings conference calls predicts a coherent
equilibrium response in corporate financial decisions:

1. +CASH (H1): Firms whose managers sound more uncertain hold more cash. The effect is
   contemporaneous and robust across all fixed-effect structures. Economic magnitude is
   small but consistent with the corporate cash literature (0.67-1.33% of DV mean).

2. -LEVERAGE (H4a, H4b): Uncertainty predicts lower FUTURE leverage (lead-concentrated).
   Both book leverage and debt-to-capital show the same pattern. The balance sheet
   adjustment lags the uncertainty signal by one quarter.

3. +R&D (H16): Uncertainty predicts HIGHER R&D spending. This is the growth-options channel:
   R&D is exploratory and option-like (capped downside, unlimited upside). Firms invest
   in R&D to explore and resolve uncertainty. Effect is large (11-17% of DV mean) and
   survives both Industry and Firm FE.

4. CAPEX DOES NOT RESPOND WITHIN-FIRM (H13): Capital expenditure — irreversible, committed
   investment — shows UncAnsMgr significance at Industry FE only, dying under Firm FE.
   This confirms Dixit & Pindyck (1994): irreversible investment is deferred under
   uncertainty. The contrast with H16 is the key insight: R&D (exploration) increases
   while capex (commitment) does not.

5. INFORMATION OPACITY AMPLIFIES (H1.2): The uncertainty-cash link is strongest for
   unrated firms — those with highest information opacity. This provides the mechanism:
   uncertainty matters most where external monitoring is weakest.

TIMING: Cash and R&D are contemporaneous. Leverage is lead-concentrated. Consistent with
an immediate response (hoard cash + explore via R&D) followed by a delayed balance sheet
adjustment (reduce debt next quarter).

  H4 (Leverage):      UncAnsCEO 1/24 marginal, UncAnsNoCEO 0/24

The CEO drives the underlying signal, not the management team. UncAnsMgr's apparent
superiority in the main tables is a coverage artifact:
  UncAnsMgr coverage: 95.8% of calls (84,484/88,205)
  UncAnsCEO coverage: 70.4% of calls (62,132/88,205)

UncAnsMgr captures the CEO signal PLUS has 39% more observations, making it a more
ROBUST predictor by virtue of coverage, not because non-CEO managers drive the result.

HONEST FRAMING: "UncAnsMgr is the preferred measure for its statistical robustness and
superior coverage. The NoCEO decomposition reveals that the CEO component carries the
primary signal. Non-CEO managers contribute only marginal Industry-FE significance,
which may reflect residual CEO contamination or genuine but weak non-CEO information."
